Thamires Andrade Lima, Ph.D.

I am a Research Assistant Professor in Chemical and Biological Engineering at Drexel University, working at the intersection of soft matter, rheology, polymer science, complex fluids, and materials characterization.

My research focuses on how processing, molecular interactions, microstructure, and external conditions control the behavior of soft materials, polymeric systems, dispersions, fiber-forming materials, and glassy or highly viscous fluids.

Research profile

I combine rheology, thermal analysis, vibrational spectroscopy, microscopy, mechanical testing, and materials processing to connect structure, dynamics, and performance in complex soft materials.

Core expertise

Rheology and complex fluids

Shear and extensional rheology, viscoelasticity, yielding, fracture-like behavior, processing–structure relationships, and high-viscosity fluids.

Polymers and soft materials

Polymer melts, dispersions, fiber systems, gels, colloids, self-assembled materials, and structure–property relationships.

Materials characterization

Raman and FTIR spectroscopy, DSC, TGA, DMA, mechanical testing, XRD, microscopy, and experimental troubleshooting.
