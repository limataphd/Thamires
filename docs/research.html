Research

My research connects experimental soft-matter science, rheology, processing, and materials characterization. The unifying goal is to understand how structure, composition, and processing determine material behavior and performance.

Soft matter and complex fluids

I study materials whose behavior is governed by mesoscale structure, molecular interactions, and processing history.

Rheology and processability

Rheology is a central tool in my work, including shear and extensional measurements of viscoelasticity, flow, yielding, deformation, and processing limits.

Structure–property relationships

I connect material composition and structure to physical properties using rheology, spectroscopy, thermal analysis, microscopy, and mechanical testing.

Future directions

I am interested in applying my soft-matter and characterization background to MXenes, two-dimensional materials, interfacial glass dynamics, confined materials, and high-pressure or extreme-condition studies.
