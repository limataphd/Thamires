About

Thamires Andrade Lima is a Research Assistant Professor in the Department of Chemical and Biological Engineering at Drexel University. Her research background spans soft matter, polymer science, complex fluids, rheology, thermal and mechanical characterization, vibrational spectroscopy, fiber processing, and structure–property relationships in materials.

Her work integrates experimental design, materials processing, and advanced characterization to understand how molecular interactions, microstructure, and processing histories control material behavior.

Professional interests

  • Soft matter, polymers, complex fluids, and rheology
  • Structure–property and process–structure–property relationships
  • Thermal, mechanical, and vibrational-spectroscopic characterization
  • Fiber spinning, additive manufacturing, and high-viscosity processing
  • Glassy and non-equilibrium materials
